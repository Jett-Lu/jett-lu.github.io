About Me

I’m Jett, an Engineering Associate Co-Op at the Ontario Ministry of Transportation, working on QA testing and automation for Laboratory Information Management Systems. My role combines software and systems to improve data accuracy, compliance, and internal tooling through scripting and CI/CD support.

My interest in tech began early through robotics clubs and hands-on tinkering. I’ve since pursued embedded systems, scripting, and full-stack development—building everything from automation tools to hardware projects.

I hold an Advanced Diploma in Computer Engineering Technology from Seneca Polytechnic, where I made the President’s Honour List multiple times, and I’m currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Software Engineering Technology at McMaster University. I work fluently in C, Python, and PowerShell, and I’m especially passionate about open-source collaboration and building secure, scalable systems.

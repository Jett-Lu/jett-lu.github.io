About Me

My name is Jett, currently an Engineering Associate Co-Op at the Ontario Ministry of Transportation, where I work hands-on with QA testing and automation for Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS). My role bridges software and systems, helping ensure data accuracy, regulatory compliance, and operational reliability across critical lab infrastructure. I contribute to streamlining CI/CD pipelines, supporting DevOps initiatives, and improving internal tools through scripting and test automation.

My passion for technology started early. Growing up, I was the kid who dismantled electronics just to figure out how they worked. I joined my school’s robotics club in elementary and again in high school, where I first got to see what code could do in the real world. That experience got me hooked on computers, and I’ve been building, fixing, and experimenting ever since—whether it was programming microcontrollers, writing scripts to automate tedious tasks, or building PCs from scratch.

I earned an Advanced Diploma in Computer Engineering Technology from Seneca Polytechnic, where I made the President’s Honour List multiple times. I’m now pursuing my Bachelor of Technology in Software Engineering Technology at McMaster University. I work fluently in C, Python, PowerShell, and embedded development environments, and I’m especially interested in open-source collaboration, security practices, and building systems that scale and last.
